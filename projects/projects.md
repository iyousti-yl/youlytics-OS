# Projects router

One folder per project: `projects/<name>/project.md` (status, area, goal, milestones, next actions, log), plus any files the project needs next to it. Plans in [planning/](../planning/planning.md) pull from every project whose status is `active`.

Add or update a project with `/project`. Status is one of `active`, `paused`, `done`. Area is `business` or `life`; a `life` project's details stay out of business files and artifacts.
