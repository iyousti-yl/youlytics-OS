# Business router (youlytics)

| Folder | What goes there |
|---|---|
| `clients/` | One folder per client: brief, notes, decks, status |
| `offers/` | What you sell: services, pricing, proposals |
| `content/` | Posts, newsletters, videos, scripts, titles and thumbnails |
| `finance/` | Revenue, costs, invoices, monthly numbers |
| `ops/` | Processes, tools, SOPs, how the business runs |

For current numbers and dashboards, use `/business-data` instead of searching.
