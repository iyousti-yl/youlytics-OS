# /wildcard

A wildcard is an option that deliberately breaks from the references while still fitting the goal.

- Default ratio: 8 of 10 options follow the references, 2 are wildcards.
- A good wildcard changes one big thing (angle, format, audience, emotion), not five small ones.
- Never break hard rules: facts stay true, brand rules stay, nothing off-limits from `CLAUDE.md`.
- Label each wildcard and say in a few words what it breaks from.
- When Issam picks one, offer to add it to the calling skill's references.
