# /grammar

Fixed-step skill, pinned to the smallest model.

1. Correct spelling, grammar and punctuation only. Keep the wording, tone and structure.
2. Keep the text's language (English, French or Dutch) and its regional spelling.
3. Return the corrected text, then a short list of what changed. If nothing needed fixing, say so.
