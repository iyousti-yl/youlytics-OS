Who we are

Trust, calm, professional

youlytics is a Belgian technology company. Today it is mainly an SAP analytics consultancy; next it will build apps and sell AI tools. The name joins Yousti and analytics.

The brand should feel like a steady expert: quiet confidence, no hype, clear numbers. Sapphire carries the trust, generous white space carries the calm, and one amber highlight shows what matters.

Use

  • Mostly mist and white, with sapphire for the logo, headers and title slides.
  • Amber for one highlight per view: the key number or the call to action. Put ink text on amber, never white.
  • Amethyst for the second series in charts and for section accents.
  • Ruby only for risks, alerts and negative numbers.

Chart order

  • Series 1 sapphire, 2 amethyst, 3 amber, 4 muted grey #8A96A8.
  • Ruby is never a regular series; it flags a problem.
  • On sapphire backgrounds, small amber text uses #E9B35A, and secondary text uses #C9D4E8.

Spacing and layout

  • 4px base scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Use nothing in between.
  • Corner radius 10px on cards and buttons, 12px on large panels.
  • Decks are 16:9 (1920×1080). One idea per slide; the headline states the takeaway.
  • Leave room: when in doubt, add space rather than another element.

Logo

youlytics

The wordmark sets "you" in 600 and "lytics" in 400, always lowercase. The mark is a y whose rising stroke ends in an amber data point.

  • Do use sapphire on light grounds and white on sapphire.
  • Do keep clear space equal to the height of the mark's dot times three.
  • Don't stretch, outline, recolour or add shadows.
  • Don't place it on busy photos or on amber.
  • Files: logo-mark.svg, logo-mark-reversed.svg.

Voice

  • Plain and precise: say what the data shows and what to do about it.
  • Calm: no exclamation marks, no hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing").
  • Trust through specifics: real numbers, named sources, clear next steps.
  • The /tone skill learns the rest from Issam's own edits.
